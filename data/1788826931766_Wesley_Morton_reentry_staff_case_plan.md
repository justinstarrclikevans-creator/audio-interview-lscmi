# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Wesley Morton

- **Participant Name:** Wesley Scott Morton
- **LS/CMI Overall Risk Level:** Medium (Score: 19)
- **Primary Criminogenic Need Domains:**

  - **Leisure / Recreation (LR):** High Risk (2/2) – High volume of unstructured idle time without organized prosocial activities.

## 2. Behavioral Dynamics & Responsivity Considerations
- **Prosocial Alignment & Responsivity Strengths:** Wesley demonstrates exceptional prosocial alignment, genuine remorse, acceptance of personal responsibility ('I had a choice'), and strong respect for authority figures. He is highly teachable and responds best to positive reinforcement rather than confrontational styles.
- **Key Behavioral Vulnerabilities:**
  - *'Cut Off' Coping Pattern:* Under high stress or feeling overwhelmed, Wesley historically abruptly disengages and quits obligations.
  - *Impulsivity:* Tendency to act without anticipating consequences.
- **Mentorship Focus:** Lacks consistent positive paternal modeling (father incarcerated). Navigator should provide strong, consistent, prosocial male mentorship and practical vocational coaching.

## 3. Dynamic Action Plan & Objectives

- **Objective:** Establish steady, hands-on employment in logistics, manufacturing, or trades while pursuing GED preparation.
- **Interventions:**
  - Enroll in forklift or HVAC hands-on vocational certification pathways.
  - Target second-chance employers with consistent daytime schedules accessible by public transit.
  - Provide job retention coaching focused on supervisor communication and stress management.

### Domain 2: Leisure & Recreation (LR)
- **Objective:** Replace unstructured idle time with structured prosocial routines.
- **Interventions:**
  - Map weekly schedule integrating work shifts, recovery meetings, and fitness/recreational activities.
  - Support involvement in structured community service or peer support groups.

### Domain 3: Structural Stability & Relapse Maintenance
- **Objective:** Maintain 10+ months sobriety and build long-term housing/mobility plan.
- **Interventions:**
  - Coordinate with Father to Father Project for driver's license restoration steps.
  - Connect with Charleston Center for outpatient peer recovery support.

## 4. Navigation & Facilitation Protocols
- **Modeling Neutrality:** Avoid power struggles or lectures. Use open questions when Wesley feels frustrated at work or check-ins.
- **CBT Skill Application:** Work through 'Decatastrophizing' worksheets when workplace conflict arises to prevent the 'Cut Off' response.
