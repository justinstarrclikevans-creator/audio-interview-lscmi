## 💼 Targeted Fair-Chance Job Matches
These employers in the Upstate region are actively hiring and are known for giving people a genuine second chance. They value hard work over perfect backgrounds.

**1. Upstate Precision Manufacturing**
- **Role:** CNC Machine Operator Trainee / Material Handler
- **Location:** Greenville / Greer, SC
- **Pay:** $18.50 – $23.00 / hr
- **Shift:** 1st & 2nd Shifts Available
- **Why it fits:** They offer on-the-job machine training, which is perfect while you are finishing your GED. They are a dedicated fair-chance partner.
- **Next Step:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=manufacturing+machine+operator&l=Greenville%2C+SC)

**2. Foothills Structural Steel & Welding**
- **Role:** Welder Apprentice / Structural Steel Helper
- **Location:** Greenville (Donaldson Center), SC
- **Pay:** $19.50 – $26.00 / hr
- **Shift:** 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM (M–F)
- **Why it fits:** If you like working with your hands, they offer an apprenticeship path and are willing to train individuals seeking career growth in skilled trades.
- **Next Step:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=structural+welder&l=Greenville%2C+SC)
