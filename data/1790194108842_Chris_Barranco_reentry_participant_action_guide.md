# My Fresh Start Action & Resource Guide: Chris Barranco

Welcome to your Fresh Start, Chris! You've already built a great foundation by living in a solid place with guys who are focused on going to work and doing the right thing. You mentioned that you like to build things—we are going to channel that energy into building a career. Because of your leg injury, our first priority is getting you medically stable, securing your ID documents, and finding a job that won't make your injury worse while you heal.

## 🏆 Your 4-Week Success Checklist

**Week 1: Focus on Health & ID**
- [ ] Apply for a replacement Social Security Card (Required for hiring).
- [ ] Make a doctor's appointment to check on your leg and manage your pain safely.

**Week 2: Transit & Mobility**
- [ ] Visit the DMV to get a printout of your driving record so we can build a reinstatement plan.
- [ ] Download the COMET bus app to map out your routes to work.

**Week 3: Job Readiness**
- [ ] Work with your Navigator to build a resume highlighting your interests in building and hands-on work.
- [ ] Complete your Turn90 CBT module on Impulse Control.

**Week 4: Employment Applications**
- [ ] Apply to 3 light-duty or assembly roles (see matches below).
- [ ] Prepare for interviews focusing on your reliability and new goals.
