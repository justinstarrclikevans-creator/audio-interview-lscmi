## 1. Motivation Tools
- **1A — Decision Making Tool (DMT):** Weighing pros and cons of staying the same vs. changing across short-term and long-term outcomes.
- **1B — Timeline:** Mapping past arrests, turning points, and patterns across time to identify recurring triggers and thinking habits.
- **1C — Responsible Decision Tree:** Tracing a high-stakes choice through irresponsible vs. responsible branches and their consequences.

## 2. Beliefs Tools
- **1D — Upgrading Beliefs:** Identifying rigid core beliefs ("should" rules, street code, entitlement) and upgrading them to flexible, pro-social beliefs that protect freedom and employment.
- **1E — Ten to One:** Generating 10 constructive perspectives or alternative interpretations for 1 limiting belief.

## 3. Thinking Tools
- **1F — Call & Response:** Rapid cognitive drill pairing an automatic high-risk thought ("Call") with an immediate pro-social counter-thought ("Response").
- **1G — Thought Tracker:** Daily self-monitoring log tracking high-risk thoughts, triggers, and replacement thoughts across the week.
- **1I — Situation-Thinking-Action-Consequences (STAC):** Core worksheet isolating the Situation ($S$), internal Thoughts ($T$), Feelings, Actions ($A$), and internal/external Consequences ($C$).
- **1K — Three to One STAC:** Taking 1 real situation and generating 3 different pro-social Thinking $\rightarrow$ Action $\rightarrow$ Consequence pathways.

## 4. Feelings & Emotion Regulation Tools
- **Emotion Regulation Social Skills (Skills 12–20):** Structured step-by-step social skills for recognizing feelings, managing anger, dealing with accusation, and expressing concern.
- **1L — Thought / Feeling Cycle:** Mapping how automatic thoughts intensify physical/emotional arousal and how shifting the thought regulates the feeling.
- **1M — Conflict Cycle:** Breaking down how stressful events trigger feelings $\rightarrow$ behaviors $\rightarrow$ adult/authority reactions, and identifying where to exit the cycle.

## 5. Problem Solving Tools
- **1N — Three Steps to Problem Solving:** (1) Stop and define the problem objectively, (2) Brainstorm at least 3 realistic options and their consequences, (3) Pick the best option and execute.

## 6. Employment Tools
- **Workplace Social Skills (Skills 21–25):** Preparing for a stressful workplace conversation, responding to supervisor criticism, asking for help, and handling workplace frustration.
- **Workplace DMT (1A), Thought Tracker (1G), and STAC (1I):** Applied specifically to job retention and coworker/supervisor triggers.
