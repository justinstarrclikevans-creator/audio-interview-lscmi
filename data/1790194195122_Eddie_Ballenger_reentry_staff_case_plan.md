# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Eddie Ballenger

## 1. Participant Overview
- **Name:** Eddie Ballenger
- **Location:** Spartanburg / Upstate SC
- **Current Status:** Enrolled in First Shift, currently in community/transitional housing. On active probation.
- **Strengths:** Strong family support (mother/siblings), positive relationship with fiancee, stable housing (loves his current placement), recognizes the value of education/employment, highly motivated to avoid re-incarceration.
- **Vulnerabilities:** Diagnosed learning disability, suspended driver's license, impulsivity, externalizes blame for past offenses, active antisocial peers.

## 2. Core Criminogenic Targets & CBT Interventions
- **Target 1: Antisocial Cognition (Blame Shifting & Impulsivity)**
  - *Intervention:* Utilize cognitive restructuring worksheets. Focus on the "Thinking Check-In." When Eddie expresses that he "had no choice," utilize Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5). Ask: *"Looking back with the wisdom you have now, what is one other way that situation could have played out?"*
- **Target 2: Antisocial Peers**
  - *Intervention:* Role-play boundary setting. Eddie noted a close friend is still engaged in crime. Discuss the "people, places, and things" that act as triggers without condemning the friend directly.
- **Target 3: Education/Employment Deficits**
  - *Intervention:* Eddie has a 10th-grade education and a learning disability. Ensure all case management materials are highly visual, verbally discussed, and paced appropriately. Connect him with GED tutoring that accommodates learning disabilities.

## 3. Barrier Removal & Stabilization
- **Transportation:** The suspended license is an immediate barrier to sustainable employment. Refer to the Upstate Fatherhood Initiative to untangle the suspension and set up public transit via Greenlink in the interim.
- **Financial Health:** Eddie relies on a Social Security check and worries about bills. Connect him to United Ministries for matched savings and financial coaching to reduce economic anxiety.

## 4. Next Steps for Navigator
- [ ] Complete referral for Upstate Fatherhood Initiative (License).
- [ ] Map out Greenlink Transit routes from his housing to prospective Upstate employers.
- [ ] Introduce the CBT "Stop and Think" module in the next 1-on-1 session.
- [ ] Coordinate with GED providers to ensure accommodations for his learning disability.
