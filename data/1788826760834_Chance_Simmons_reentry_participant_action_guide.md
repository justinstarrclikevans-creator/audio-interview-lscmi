## Local Community Resources & Support

### 1. Driver's License & Family Support
* **Center for Fathering / Father to Father Project**
  * **Address:** 4925 Lacross Rd, North Charleston, SC 29406
  * **Phone:** (843) 744-2126
  * **Services:** Guidance on converting beginner's permit to full driver's license, driver education resources, and family stability coaching.
  * **Action Step:** Call or visit to schedule an intake for driver license restoration support.
  * [Visit Website](https://scfathersandfamilies.com) | [Get Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=4925+Lacross+Rd,+North+Charleston,+SC+29406)

### 2. Driver's License Reinstatement & DMV Services
* **SCDMV Regional Service Center**
  * **Address:** 3790 Leeds Ave, North Charleston, SC 29405
  * **Phone:** (803) 896-5000
  * **Services:** Official driving records, permit testing, and license issue processing.
  * **Action Step:** Work with your Navigator to review official driving records before scheduling road tests.
  * [Visit Website](https://scdmvonline.com/Driver-Services/Reinstatement) | [Get Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=3790+Leeds+Ave,+North+Charleston,+SC+29405)

### 3. Public Transportation
* **CARTA Transit Authority**
  * **Phone:** (843) 724-7420
  * **Services:** Fixed route bus service and express workforce shuttle connections across Charleston and North Charleston.
  * **Action Step:** Review bus route maps for connections from Johns Island to job locations.
  * [Visit Website](https://ridecarta.com)

### 4. Substance Wellness & Relapse Prevention
* **Charleston Center (DAODAS)**
  * **Address:** 5 Charleston Center Dr, Charleston, SC 29401
