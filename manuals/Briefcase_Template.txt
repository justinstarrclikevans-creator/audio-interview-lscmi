# Unified Single-Document Briefcase Specification (No Gates)

> [!CAUTION]
> **STRICT ANTI-HALLUCINATION & ZERO-EXAMPLE-LEAKAGE POLICY**
> 1. **Never pull or cite examples, names, or dialogue from any skill, manual, or reference file** as evidence in a participant or staff evaluation.
> 2. **Every claim, coaching observation, or behavioral match MUST be grounded in a verbatim quote** (`6–35 words`) with an `[MM:SS]` timestamp from the actual session transcript or explicit intake record.
> 3. Any quote that fails deterministic substring/trigram verification against the input transcript is immediately stripped.


## 1. Design Rules
- **No Gates:** All 4-week readiness gates and locked progression bars are removed. The Briefcase is **one unified, interactive document** with dropdowns and options.
- **Auto-Populated from Loaded Information:** Automatically pre-fills from the Screening Consultation, Apricot imports, LS/CMI interview, and Case Management notes.
- **Conditional Medical Logic (CRITICAL):**
  - First ask/check: **`has_chronic_health_condition`** (`No Chronic Conditions` vs. `Has Chronic Condition Requiring Treatment/Daily Medication`).
  - **If `No Chronic Conditions`:** Automatically set medical treatment/medication status to `N/A — No Chronic Conditions`, hide medical appointment scheduling prompts, and **NEVER ask or flag whether the participant needs medical appointments**.
  - **If `Has Chronic Condition`:** Require dropdown verification of whether the condition is treated and the participant has access to necessary daily medications (`Treated & Has Daily Meds`, `Needs Medical Appointment`, `Needs Rx Refill / Insurance Barrier`).

## 2. The 9 Required Items for the First Shift Waitlist
A participant is automatically flagged as **`First Shift Waitlist Ready`** when all 9 items below are in a green/compliant state (or `Not Applicable` where conditional):

| # | Waitlist Requirement | Dropdown Options | Waitlist-Ready Values |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **State ID or Driver's License (DL)** | `Valid Driver's License`, `Valid State ID (No DL)`, `DL Suspended (Has State ID)`, `DL Suspended (No ID)`, `Applied / Receipt in Hand`, `Missing` | `Valid Driver's License`, `Valid State ID (No DL)`, `DL Suspended (Has State ID)` |
| **2** | **Social Security Card** | `Physical Card Verified`, `Replacement Receipt Verified`, `Applied / Ordered`, `Missing` | `Physical Card Verified`, `Replacement Receipt Verified` |
| **3** | **Birth Certificate** | `Certified Copy Verified`, `Ordered / In Transit`, `Missing` | `Certified Copy Verified` |
| **4** | **Chronic Health Issues Treated &/or Access to Necessary Daily Medications** | `No Chronic Conditions (N/A)`, `Treated & Has Daily Medications`, `Needs Medical Appointment`, `Needs Medication Access / Refill` | `No Chronic Conditions (N/A)`, `Treated & Has Daily Medications` |
| **5** | **Stable Address / Housing** | `Stable Housing Verified`, `Approved Transitional Housing`, `Temporary / Couch Surfing (Needs Stabilization)`, `Unhoused / Shelter` | `Stable Housing Verified`, `Approved Transitional Housing` |
| **6** | **Reliable Phone Number** | `Active & Reliable Phone Verified`, `Temporary / Wi-Fi Only (Needs Phone)`, `No Phone / Disconnected` | `Active & Reliable Phone Verified` |
| **7** | **Gone to DMV to Figure Out Why DL is Suspended** *(Conditional on DL status)* | `Not Applicable (DL Valid or Never Issued)`, `Completed DMV Inquiry — Reinstatement Plan Obtained`, `Scheduled / Pending DMV Visit`, `Needs DMV Visit` | `Not Applicable (DL Valid or Never Issued)`, `Completed DMV Inquiry — Reinstatement Plan Obtained` |
| **8** | **Contact Made with Child Support (if applicable)** | `Not Applicable (No Child Support Case)`, `Contact Made & Compliance Plan Active`, `Current / Paid Up`, `Needs Contact / Unresolved Warrant Risk` | `Not Applicable (No Child Support Case)`, `Contact Made & Compliance Plan Active`, `Current / Paid Up` |
| **9** | **Supervision Information** | `Not Under Supervision (Maxed Out / Non-Supervised)`, `Active Supervision — Officer & Conditions Logged`, `Pending Charges — PO/PD Written Clearance Received`, `Missing Supervision Info / Unresolved Court Conflict` | `Not Under Supervision (Maxed Out / Non-Supervised)`, `Active Supervision — Officer & Conditions Logged`, `Pending Charges — PO/PD Written Clearance Received` |

## 3. Additional Unified Briefcase Dropdown Sections (In the Same Document)
- **Work Readiness & Documents:** W-9 / Payroll Setup (`Completed`, `In Progress`, `Not Started`), Resume (`Completed`, `Drafted`, `Not Started`), Birth/ID Copies Uploaded to Participant Folder (`Yes`, `Pending`).
- **SkillCat Pathway & Trade Certifications:** Selected Pathway (`HVAC`, `Electrical`, `Plumbing`, `Facilities Maintenance`, `Commercial/Industrial Safety`, `Undecided`), Current Module Status (`On Track`, `Ahead`, `Stalled — Flagged`, `Not Started`).
- **Transportation & Benefits:** Primary Commute Plan (`Personal Vehicle`, `Bus Pass / Public Transit`, `Confirmed Ride`, `Needs Transit Plan`), SNAP/Medicaid/Benefits (`Active`, `Applied`, `Not Needed`, `Needs Application`).
