# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Christian (Chris) Shawn Isham

## 1. Case Summary & Triage
Chris is currently engaging with supervision and living in a stable, prosocial environment (Oxford House). However, he faces **immediate physical and administrative barriers** to work. He has a broken leg causing severe radiating pain, lacks a Social Security Card (preventing I-9 verification), and has a suspended driver's license. Furthermore, he is currently prescribed unknown medications for his leg, which poses a relapse risk given his self-admitted history of drug problems.

## 2. Core Criminogenic Targets
*   **Substance Use / Relapse Risk:** Taking active pain medication for a broken leg while recovering from past substance abuse. Requires a strong relapse prevention plan and medical oversight.
*   **Education / Employment:** 9th-grade education; no GED. Currently lacks a valid ID/SSN card to begin work. Physical limitations require light-duty or sedentary job placements.
*   **Impulsivity:** Self-reported getting into trouble "by accident a lot."

## 3. Facilitation & Coaching Strategies (Turn90 Framework)
*   **Modeling Neutrality (1.5):** Chris expressed that "police just be ridiculous about some things." *Action:* Do not challenge this. Respond neutrally: "It sounds frustrating when you feel you're being messed with. What's the best way to handle those situations so they don't threaten your freedom?"
*   **Managing Buy-In (3.2):** Link compliance (like getting his SSN and dealing with the DMV) to his goal of earning his own money and building a self-sufficient life without relying on his family, especially given the choppy relationship with his mother.
*   **CBT Workbook Focus:** Assign modules on *Impulse Control* and *Consequential Thinking* to address his pattern of "accidentally" getting into trouble.

## 4. 30-Day Milestone Plan
*   **Week 1 (Stabilization):** Request a replacement Social Security Card. Connect with Cooperative Health for a follow-up on his leg and pain management plan.
*   **Week 2 (Transit & Documentation):** Pull his driving record at SCDMV to understand reinstatement requirements. Map out COMET bus routes from his Oxford House to the Turn90 center and potential light-duty job sites.
*   **Week 3 (Job Prep):** Resume building focusing on his interest in "building" and hands-on work, pivoting toward light assembly or records management while his leg heals.
*   **Week 4 (Placement):** Apply for light-industrial, seated, or low-impact roles (e.g., Schneider Electric, Robert Half) that accommodate his physical recovery.
