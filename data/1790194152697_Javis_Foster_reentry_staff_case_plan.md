# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Javis Foster

## 1. Immediate Stabilization & Crisis Intervention
**CRITICAL ACTION:** Javis disclosed suicidal ideation ("Sometimes it comes contemplate suicide"). 
*   **Action:** Immediate mental health triage. Create a safety plan today. Provide crisis hotline information (988) and refer for a clinical assessment.
*   **Relapse Prevention:** He has been clean for 9 months from opiates/benzos. The stress of pending charges and financial strain is a high trigger for relapse. Build a structured relapse prevention plan.

## 2. CBT & Facilitation Focus (Turn90 Standards)
*   **Target:** Externalization of Blame & Minimization.
*   **Strategy (Modeling Neutrality 1.5):** Javis minimizes his actions (e.g., claiming lack of sleep caused his crime, minimizing his evading charge). Do not debate his version of events. Use open-ended, reflective questions: *"You mentioned you ran through someone's house to get away. Walk me through what was going through your mind in that exact moment."*
*   **Managing Buy-In (3.2):** Link every requirement to his goal of providing for his child and partner. *"Getting your GED and addressing these pending charges head-on is the fastest way to get to that $20/hr mark to support your family."*

## 3. Barrier Removal & Navigation
*   **Education:** Connect Javis to GED classes immediately. He has an active license, SSN, and birth certificate, which removes a major hurdle for employment.
*   **Legal:** He has pending charges (Burglary 2nd, Evading). Ensure he is connected to his public defender and complies strictly with any pretrial supervision conditions to prevent pre-trial detention.
*   **Employment:** Prep him for fair-chance manufacturing roles in the Upstate (Greenville/Greer) area. Since he has stable transportation (active license), regional commutes are viable.

## 4. Weekly Milestones for Navigator
*   **Week 1:** Mental health safety plan, referral to counseling, and connection to GED prep program.
*   **Week 2:** Review legal status of pending charges; begin CBT workbook exercises on "Thinking Traps" (minimization).
*   **Week 3:** Mock interviews focusing on how to explain past convictions and pending charges professionally.
*   **Week 4:** Submit applications to Fair-Chance employers in Upstate SC.
