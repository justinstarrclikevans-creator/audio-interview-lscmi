## 4-Week Action Plan

* **Week 1: Foundations & Health Care**
  * Attend initial Re-entry Navigation session.
  * Contact Fetter Health Care Network to schedule intake.
  * Confirm probation meeting schedule.
* **Week 2: Education & Skills Preparation**
  * Enroll in math GED practice/tutoring sessions.
  * Complete resume updates featuring HVAC and mechanical experience.
* **Week 3: Job Applications & Interviews**
  * Submit applications to Lowcountry Framing and Palmetto Logistics.
  * Practice CBT workplace conflict de-escalation scenarios with navigator.
* **Week 4: Onboarding & Long-Term CDL Pathway**
  * Review job offer details and work schedule.
  * Establish a clear 90-day drug-free milestone plan towards future CDL training.
