# FIRST SHIFT BRIEFCASE STRUCTURE & DOMAINS

## Section 1: Core Identification & Stability
- State ID
- Social Security Card
- Birth Certificate
- Driver's License (SCDMV status & reinstatement plan)
- Reliable Phone Number
- Professional Email Address
- Library Card
- Bank Account (Direct Deposit setup)
- Child Support Contact/Status Reviewed
- Transportation Plan (Bus/Van/Independent)
- Housing Plan / Stable Address (60+ days)

## Section 2: Employment Readiness
- Ferguson Career Aptitude Test
- Career Interest Discussion
- Professional Email Created
- Resume Completed (Master Resume in Builder)
- Workplace References Identified (2-3 verifiable references)
- Interview Practice Completed (Spoken 60-second conviction explanation & behavioral questions)
- Interview Clothing
- Work Tools / Clothing (Steel-toe boots, PPE)
- Job Applications Submitted

## Section 3: Industry Credentials & Training
- OSHA-10 Safety Certification
- Forklift Certification
- Home Depot Path to Pro Credentials
- Trade Foundations (Carpentry, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Drywall, Paint, Welding)

## Section 4: Health & Wellness Foundation
- Health Insurance / Coverage Plan
- Welvista Referral Reviewed (Prescription assistance)
- Primary Care / Doctor Visit
- Vision Appointment (Glasses / Eye exam)
- Prescription Needs Reviewed (Maintenance medications)
- Mental Health Referral (if requested)
- Substance Recovery Support Plan (if applicable)

## Section 5: Financial & Life Stability
- Bank Account Opened
- Budget Worksheet Completed
- Understanding of Paychecks & Taxes (Deductions, W-4 withholdings, net pay)
- Savings Goal Identified
- Credit Report Reviewed
- Child Support Questions Reviewed
- Probation / Parole Obligations Reviewed

## Section 6: Career Planning & Long-Term Growth
- 90-Day Goal Worksheet
- Target Industry Identified
- Entry-Level Job Goal Identified
- Next Credential Goal Identified
- 6-Month Goal Written
- Long-Term Wage Goal Identified
