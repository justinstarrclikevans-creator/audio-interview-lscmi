## 3. 4-Week Step-by-Step Milestone Checklist

- [ ] **Week 1: Stabilization & Transit Setup**
  - Complete intake with Charleston Center for recovery support.
  - Map CARTA bus routes from transitional housing to job match locations.
  - Secure CARTA bus pass through Turn90 staff.

- [ ] **Week 2: Physical Health & Application Launch**
  - Complete healthcare checkup at Fetter Health Care for anxiety medication routine.
  - Submit applications to Palmetto Logistics, Carolina Marine, and Builders FirstSource.
  - Practice neutral communication and de-escalation strategies during mock job interviews.

- [ ] **Week 3: Driver's License & Interview Phase**
  - Attend consultation with Father to Father Project for driver's license restoration steps.
  - Attend job interviews; utilize CBT pause techniques if feeling stressed or disrespected.
  - Maintain 100% compliance with Turn90 and probation schedule.

- [ ] **Week 4: Onboarding & Sustained Growth**
  - Complete job onboarding and establish daily transit routing for work.
  - Create weekly budget plan with Navigator to manage paycheck savings.
  - Continue participation in outpatient peer recovery groups.
