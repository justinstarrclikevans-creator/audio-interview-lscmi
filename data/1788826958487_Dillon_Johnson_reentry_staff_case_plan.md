# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Dillon Johnson

## 1. Assessment Overview & Criminogenic Profile
- **Overall Risk Level:** High
- **Primary Criminogenic Targets:**
  1. **Education/Employment:** Unemployed, history of workplace altercations, job loss driven by authority conflict.
  2. **Alcohol/Drug Problem:** Heavy marijuana/edible use as an unhealthy coping mechanism leading to distress and reckless spending.
  3. **Procriminal Attitude/Orientation:** 'Cut-Off' thinking, rapid escalation to verbal/property aggression under perceived slights.
- **Non-Dynamic Baseline:** Justice involvement beginning at age 19, multiple incarcerations, active probation supervision.

## 2. CBT & Facilitation Strategy (Turn90 Standards)
- **Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5):** Staff must refrain from debating or challenging anti-social or externalizing statements. Use neutral curiosity (e.g., 'When you responded that way to your supervisor, how did that impact your paycheck?') to highlight consequences.
- **Managing Resistance & Buy-In (Standard 3.2 & 18.0):** Directly connect compliance, distress tolerance, and self-regulation to Dillon's stated goals of staying out of jail, earning income, and keeping his housing.
- **Cognitive Restructuring Targets:** Interrupt 'Cut-Off' thinking during perceived disrespect using de-escalation scripts and time-out protocols.

## 3. Stabilization Roadmap
- **Substance Recovery:** Enroll in outpatient support at Charleston Center (DAODAS) to build structured coping skills replacing daily marijuana use.
- **Healthcare Maintenance:** Ensure continuity of anxiety/depression medication (Hydroxyzine) via Fetter Health Care / Welvista.
- **Transportation Setup:** Map out CARTA transit routes from transitional housing to prospective work sites.

## 4. Supervision & Legal Compliance
- Maintain weekly communication with PO; reinforce that program participation is Dillon's key mechanism for avoiding probation revocation.
