## Community Resources & Referrals

### 1. Free Prescription Medication (Hypertension)
* **Welvista (Statewide Prescription Assistance)**
  * **Services:** Free maintenance medication delivered to your door for uninsured SC residents.
  * **Phone:** (803) 933-9183
  * **Website:** [Visit Welvista Online](https://welvista.org)
  * **Action Step:** Fill out the enrollment form with your Navigator today so your blood pressure medication is delivered without delay.

## Your 4-Week Step-by-Step Milestone Plan

* [ ] **Week 1: Health & Legal Triage**
  * Complete and submit Welvista prescription application.
  * Contact Father to Father Project to schedule an intake regarding child support and license reinstatement.
  * Confirm work shift schedules with probation officer to ensure full compliance with 9:00 PM curfew.

* [ ] **Week 2: Job Applications & Initial Interviews**
  * Submit applications to Palmetto Logistics and Lowcountry Framing.
  * Attend interview prep sessions with your Re-entry Navigator.
  * Obtain official driver record from SCDMV Leeds Ave location.

* [ ] **Week 3: Employment Start & Housing Planning**
  * Start position at selected day-shift employer.
  * Meet with Origin SC housing counselor to set up savings budget.
  * Attend first follow-up meeting with Father to Father Project for court review setup.

* [ ] **Week 4: Stability & Long-Term Goal Setting**
  * Complete first two weeks of employment successfully.
  * Review child support payment agreement status.
  * Map out steps and prerequisites for future CDL-B training enrollment.
