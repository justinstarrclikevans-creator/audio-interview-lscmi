## 2. Facilitation & Intervention Strategies (CBT Standards)
* **Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5):** Staff must remain completely non-reactive during outbursts or anti-social statements. Avoid debating Dillon or challenging authority defiance head-on. Use non-judgmental reflective statements (e.g., *"When you respond to a supervisor that way, how does that affect your goal of staying off probation?"*).
* **Managing Resistance & Buy-In (Standards 3.2 & 18.0):** De-escalate authority friction by validating his feelings of frustration without validating destructive actions. Frame every program rule, employment task, and probation requirement in terms of his personal self-interest (retaining income, staying out of jail, securing independence).
* **De-escalation Protocol:** When Dillon shows signs of emotional volatility or triggers (feeling ignored/slighted), enforce a calm time-out space and guide him through CBT thought-restructuring workbooks to identify the "Cut Off" thinking cycle.
