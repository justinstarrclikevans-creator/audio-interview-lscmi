## 1. Key Community Resources & Referrals

### Transit & Getting to Work
- **CARTA (Charleston Area Regional Transportation Authority)**
  - **Services:** Fixed bus routes and workforce shuttles across Downtown, North Charleston, and West Ashley.
  - **Address:** Regional Stops throughout Charleston Tri-County
  - **Phone:** (843) 724-7420
  - **Link:** [Visit Website](https://ridecarta.com) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=Charleston+Area+Regional+Transportation+Authority)
  - **Action Step:** Work with your Navigator to obtain a monthly bus pass and map your route to employer sites.

### Healthcare & Prescription Maintenance
- **Welvista (Prescription Assistance)**
  - **Services:** Free maintenance prescription delivery for uninsured South Carolina residents (e.g., Hydroxyzine refill support).
  - **Phone:** (803) 933-9183
  - **Link:** [Visit Website](https://welvista.org)
  - **Action Step:** Submit an application with Navigator support to ensure uninterrupted access to maintenance meds.

- **Fetter Health Care Network**
  - **Services:** Sliding-scale primary medical, behavioral health, and dental care.
  - **Address:** 51 Nassau St, Charleston, SC 29403
  - **Phone:** (843) 722-4112
  - **Link:** [Visit Website](https://fetterhealthcare.org) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=51+Nassau+St,+Charleston,+SC+29403)
  - **Action Step:** Schedule a primary health checkup and routine prescription review.

### Recovery & Relapse Prevention
- **Charleston Center (DAODAS)**
  - **Services:** Intensive outpatient support, peer recovery support, and relapse prevention counseling.
  - **Address:** 5 Charleston Center Dr, Charleston, SC 29401
  - **Phone:** (843) 958-3300
  - **Link:** [Visit Website](https://charlestoncounty.org/departments/charleston-center/) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=5+Charleston+Center+Dr,+Charleston,+SC+29401)
  - **Action Step:** Connect with peer recovery specialists to build a solid relapse prevention plan for stress management.

### Driver's License Reinstatement
- **Center for Fathering / Father to Father Project**
  - **Services:** Driver's license restoration support, child support modification assistance, and legal barrier reduction.
  - **Address:** 4925 Lacross Rd, North Charleston, SC 29406
  - **Phone:** (843) 744-2126
  - **Link:** [Visit Website](https://scfathersandfamilies.com) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=4925+Lacross+Rd,+North+Charleston,+SC+29406)
  - **Action Step:** Meet with a case manager to check DMV record status and create a license recovery roadmap.
