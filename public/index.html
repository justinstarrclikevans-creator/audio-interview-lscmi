Form W-9: Request for Taxpayer Identification Number and Certification

Department of the Treasury • Internal Revenue Service (IRS Rev. March 2024)
GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS: Give Form to the requester. Do not send to the IRS. Required for Turn90 participant stipend / payroll setup.
Requester's Name & Address:
Turn90, Inc.
3765 Leeds Ave, North Charleston, SC 29405
Part I: Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)

Enter your TIN in the appropriate box. The TIN provided must match the name given on line 1 to avoid backup withholding. For individuals, this is generally your social security number (SSN).

Part II: Certification

Under penalties of perjury, I certify that:

  1. The number shown on this form is my correct taxpayer identification number (or I am waiting for a number to be issued to me); and
  2. I am not subject to backup withholding because: (a) I am exempt from backup withholding, or (b) I have not been notified by the IRS that I am subject to backup withholding, or (c) the IRS has notified me that I am no longer subject to backup withholding; and
  3. I am a U.S. citizen or other U.S. person; and
  4. The FATCA code(s) entered on this form (if any) indicating that I am exempt from FATCA reporting is correct.
Typing your legal name constitutes an official legally binding electronic signature under federal law (E-SIGN Act).
