## 2. Recommended Fair-Chance Employer Matches

### 1. CMC Steel South Carolina
* **Role:** Mill Operator Trainee / Material Handler  
* **Pay Rate:** $19.00 – $24.50 / hr  
* **Shift:** Day Shift (7:00 AM – 3:30 PM)  
* **Why It Fits:** Excellent match for your physical fitness, team discipline, and focus on steady career advancement. Located directly in the Columbia/Cayce transit area with full safety gear provided.  
* **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.cmc.com/en-us/careers)

### 2. Palmetto State Distribution Center
* **Role:** Order Selector / Warehouse Associate  
* **Pay Rate:** $17.50 – $21.50 / hr  
* **Shift:** 1st Shift (7:00 AM – 3:30 PM)  
* **Why It Fits:** Consistent first-shift schedule, weekly pay, fair-chance background policy, accessible via COMET bus lines.  
* **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=warehouse+order+selector&l=West+Columbia%2C+SC)

### 3. Carolina Industrial Maintenance Group
* **Role:** Maintenance Technician Apprentice / Commercial Groundskeeper  
* **Pay Rate:** $16.50 – $19.50 / hr  
* **Shift:** Day Shift (7:30 AM – 4:00 PM, M–F)  
* **Why It Fits:** Paid training, background-friendly team environment, steady daytime hours ideal for halfway house reporting.  
* **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=maintenance+technician&l=Columbia%2C+SC)
