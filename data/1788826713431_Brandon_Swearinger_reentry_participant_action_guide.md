### 3. Fair-Chance Career Opportunities in Columbia
These local fair-chance employers value a strong work ethic, offer day shifts, and provide hands-on training:

1. **Carolina Industrial Maintenance Group**  
   * **Role:** Maintenance Technician Apprentice / Commercial Grounds  
   * **Pay:** $16.50 – $19.50 / hr (Day Shift: 7:30 AM – 4:00 PM, M–F)  
   * **Why it fits:** Hands-on maintenance training, paid on-the-job instruction, clear daily structure.  
   * **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=maintenance+technician&l=Columbia%2C+SC)

2. **CMC Steel South Carolina**  
   * **Role:** Mill Operator Trainee / Material Handler  
   * **Pay:** $19.00 – $24.50 / hr (Day Shift: 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM)  
   * **Why it fits:** Competitive pay, full healthcare benefits, excellent entry point into industrial trade work.  
   * **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.cmc.com/en-us/careers)

3. **Palmetto State Distribution Center**  
   * **Role:** Order Selector / Warehouse Associate  
   * **Pay:** $17.50 – $21.50 / hr (1st Shift: 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM)  
   * **Why it fits:** Reliable weekly pay, structured environment, fair-chance background policy.  
   * **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=warehouse+order+selector&l=West+Columbia%2C+SC)
