# PROBATION AND PAROLE TREATMENT PLANNER & CRIMINOGENIC NEEDS GUIDELINES

## Core Criminogenic Need Domains (RNR Framework)
In accordance with the LS/CMI and the Probation and Parole Treatment Planner, interventions must directly target the participant's dynamic criminogenic risk/need factors. Criminal history is static and must NEVER be selected as a top dynamic target for intervention.

### 1. Antisocial / Pro-Criminal Attitudes & Thinking (Procriminal Cognitions)
- **Clinical Definition:** Thoughts, values, beliefs, and rationalizations that justify law-breaking, anti-social conduct, defiance of authority, or impulsive retaliation.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "How you view rules, authority, and accountability, and catching automatic negative thoughts before they turn into impulsive actions."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Cognitive Behavioral Training (CBT): Teaching the Cognitive Triangle (Thoughts -> Feelings -> Actions).
  - Identification and reframing of cognitive distortions (e.g., victim stance, jumping to conclusions, blaming system).
  - Practice using the 5-second pause and SODAS decision-making method in workplace scenarios.

### 2. Antisocial Associates & Peer Influence
- **Clinical Definition:** Social networks comprised of individuals engaged in criminal conduct or substance use, combined with an absence of prosocial supportive peers.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "The people you spend time with and who influences your daily choices. Building a supportive circle that protects your fresh start."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Social network mapping: Identifying high-risk individuals and setting strict interpersonal boundaries.
  - Development of refusal skills and strategies for disengaging from old neighborhood crews.
  - Engagement with positive workplace mentors and peer recovery specialists.

### 3. Substance Abuse / Addictive Behaviors
- **Clinical Definition:** Recurrent alcohol or drug use resulting in legal, occupational, health, or interpersonal dysfunction.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "Managing triggers, cravings, and stress without relying on drugs or alcohol."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Development of a written Relapse Prevention Plan detailing triggers, high-risk times, and emergency contacts.
  - Active participation in recovery support groups (12-step, Celebrate Recovery, SMART Recovery).
  - Random accountability screens and connecting with clinical treatment if indicated.

### 4. Family / Marital Relationships & Domestic Stability
- **Clinical Definition:** High-conflict, unsupportive, or criminogenic family dynamics; domestic discord that disrupts emotional stability.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "Building healthy communication and managing conflict with family, partners, and children so your home life supports your job."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Communication skills training: Assertive 'I' statements vs. aggressive/passive reactions.
  - Structured visitation schedules and establishing clear financial/child support responsibility.
  - Establishing boundaries with high-conflict family members during the work week.

### 5. Education / Employment Deficits & Workplace Retention
- **Clinical Definition:** Lack of marketable skills, unstable employment history, difficulty adapting to workplace supervision, or chronic unemployment.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "Developing job skills, understanding workplace expectations, and building a reliable track record to earn steady income."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Master resume development and practiced explanation of criminal background.
  - Hands-on skills training in construction/manufacturing and earning recognized industry credentials.
  - Coaching on managing supervisor feedback and job site conflict resolution.

### 6. Leisure, Recreation & Prosocial Time Use
- **Clinical Definition:** Unstructured, unmonitored idle time; lack of involvement in prosocial recreational, athletic, or community activities.
- **Participant-Facing Definition:** "Filling your free time outside of work with positive, healthy activities that keep you out of trouble."
- **Evidence-Based Interventions (Probation Planner):**
  - Scheduling daily routines and time-budgeting for evenings and weekends.
  - Connecting with local community centers, sports leagues, fitness gyms, or faith communities.
