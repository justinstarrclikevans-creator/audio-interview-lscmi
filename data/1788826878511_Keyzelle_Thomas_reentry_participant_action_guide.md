## Local Fair-Chance Employment Matches

1. **Palmetto State Distribution Center**  
   - **Role:** Order Selector / Package Handler  
   - **Pay Rate:** $17.50 – $21.50 / hr  
   - **Shift:** 1st Shift (7:00 AM – 3:30 PM)  
   - **Why It Fits:** Predictable daytime hours align well with sleep/health routine and evening youth sports coaching.  
   - **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=warehouse+order+selector&l=West+Columbia%2C+SC)

2. **Carolina Industrial Maintenance Group**  
   - **Role:** Maintenance Technician Apprentice  
   - **Pay Rate:** $16.50 – $19.50 / hr  
   - **Shift:** Day Shift (7:30 AM – 4:00 PM, M–F)  
   - **Why It Fits:** Leverages technical college degree credentials in an active, supportive team environment.  
   - **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=maintenance+technician&l=Columbia%2C+SC)

3. **Schneider Electric Columbia Facility**  
   - **Role:** Electro-Mechanical Assembler / Warehouse Associate  
   - **Pay Rate:** $18.50 – $22.50 / hr  
   - **Shift:** 1st Shift (6:30 AM – 3:00 PM)  
   - **Why It Fits:** Great fit for technical skills; supportive second-chance employer with full benefits.  
   - **Apply:** [Apply Online / View Careers Page](https://www.se.com/us/en/about-us/careers/overview.jsp)
