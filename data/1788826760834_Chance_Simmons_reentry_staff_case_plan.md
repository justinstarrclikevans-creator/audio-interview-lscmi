# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Chance Simmons

## Executive Summary & Assessment Profile
Participant Chance Simmons is a 40-year-old male under SCDC Reentry Parole Supervision returning to Charleston after serving nearly 20 continuous years of incarceration from age 17. His score-based risk level is **HIGH (23/43)**, heavily weighted by static criminal history and dynamic deficits in **Education/Employment (7/9)**, **Leisure/Recreation (2/2)**, and **Companions (3/4)**. 

Crucially, Chance presents with exceptional protective strengths: **0/4 in Procriminal Attitude** (demonstrates genuine remorse, full accountability, and proactive seeking of psychological counseling) and **0/4 in Family/Marital** (resides in a highly supportive, stable parental home on Johns Island). He has no active acute housing or substance crises.

## Facilitation & CBT Coaching Strategy
- **Modeling Neutrality & Relational Tone:** Adopt an appreciative, encouraging, peer-level coaching tone. Chance is highly sensitive to authority and eager to please; avoid confrontational tactics or power struggles.
- **Targeting Dynamic Criminogenic Needs:**
  1. *Education/Employment:* Transition GED foundation into practical workplace habits, digital literacy, and first-ever community job placement.
  2. *Leisure/Recreation:* Restructure idle TV time into structured daily routines, trade training, and family-oriented prosocial activities.
  3. *Companions:* Insulate against negative neighborhood peer networks by reinforcing family protective factors and workplace social integration.
- **Impatience Management:** When minor impatience surfaces around job search pacing, validate his eagerness to earn while using CBT thought-mapping to highlight incremental progress.

## 90-Day Action Roadmap
1. **Weeks 1–2 (Stabilization & Licensing):** Connect with Father to Father Project for beginner's permit advancement to full driver's license; set up CARTA transit schedules from Johns Island.
2. **Weeks 3–6 (Digital Literacy & Job Preparation):** Conduct 1-on-1 digital application practice; submit tailored applications to local Fair-Chance trade and logistics partners.
3. **Weeks 7–12 (Placement & Maintenance):** Secure entry-level employment; monitor initial supervisor interactions; establish ongoing relapse prevention check-ins.
