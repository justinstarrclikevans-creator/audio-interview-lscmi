 CASE  MANAGEMENT  BRIEF 
 The  Case  Management  Brief  is  an  internal,  staff-facing  document  completed  after  LS/CMI  and 
 TJ  New  Hire  Intake.  It  prepares  the  Program  Manager  for  effective,  structured  case  planning 
 and  stabilization. 
 The  Brief  includes: 
 1.  Risk  Profile 
 2.  Behavioral  Indicators 
 3.  Pattern  Over  Time 
 4.  Acute  /  Emergency  Needs 
 5.  Structural  Stability  Factors 
 6.  Legal  &  Placement  Constraints 
 7.  Responsivity  &  Engagement  Profile 
 Length:  2  pages  maximum 
 Tone:  Interpretive,  concise,  professional 
 Purpose:  Translation  layer  between  assessment  and  case  planning 
 DETAILED  EXPLANATION  OF  EACH  SECTION 
 1.  Risk  Profile 
 Source:  LS/CMI 
 Purpose:  Anchor  case  plan  direction 
 Include: 
 ●  Overall  risk  level 
 ●  List  top  3  criminogenic  domains  in  risk  order  with  top  being  number  1 
 ●  Brief  domain  indicators  (1–2  per  domain) 
 This  section  defines: 
 What  must  be  addressed  to  reduce  recidivism  risk. 
 This  is  not  a  score  report.  It  is  a  structured  summary  of  the  most  important  drivers. 
 2.  Behavioral  Indicators 
 Source:  LS/CMI  interpretation 
 Purpose:  Translate  domains  into  observable  behavior 
 Behavioral  indicators  describe  how  risk  manifests  in  real  life. 
 Include: 
 ●  Typical  triggers 
 ●  Escalation  tendencies 
 ●  Decision-making  patterns 
 ●  Authority  response  style 
 ●  Substance  relapse  patterns  (if  applicable) 
 This  section  answers: 
 How  does  this  risk  show  up  day-to-day? 
 Behavioral  indicators  are  used  to: 
 ●  Guide  pattern  menu  selection 
 ●  Anticipate  friction  points 
 ●  Inform  coaching  approach 
 They  are  not  case  plan  goals. 
 3.  Pattern  Over  Time 
 Source:  LS/CMI  +  justice  trajectory 
 Purpose:  Understand  longitudinal  cycle 
 Include: 
 ●  Age  at  first  justice  involvement  (if  relevant) 
 ●  Number/frequency  of  incarcerations 
 ●  Recidivism  timing  patterns  (e.g.,  relapse  within  60  days  post-release) 
 ●  Escalation  or  stability  trends 
 ●  Supervision  violation  history 
 This  section  answers: 
 What  cycle  are  we  interrupting? 
 This  helps  determine  urgency  and  intensity. 
 4.  Acute  /  Emergency  Needs 
 Source:  Intake 
 Purpose:  Immediate  stabilization 
 Include  only  urgent  issues  requiring  early  intervention: 
 ●  Housing  crisis 
 ●  Active  substance  use 
 ●  Mental  health  instability 
 ●  Court  date  within  30  days 
 ●  Transportation  crisis  affecting  attendance 
 ●  Safety  concerns 
 This  section  answers: 
 What  must  be  addressed  immediately? 
 If  this  section  is  empty,  state: 
 “No  acute  needs  identified  at  intake.” 
 5.  Stability  Factors 
 Source:  Intake  (interpreted) 
 Purpose:  Anticipate  stressors  affecting  progress 
 Include: 
 ●  Housing  stability  level 
 ●  Driver’s  license  status 
 ●  Education  credential  status 
 ●  Recent  substance  use 
 ●  Employment  history  pattern 
 ●  Supervision  intensity 
 These  are  not  necessarily  criminogenic  drivers,  but  they  affect  stress,  stability,  and  sequencing 
 of  support. 
 This  section  answers: 
 What  structural  barriers  may  affect  progress? 
 6.  Legal  &  Placement  Constraints 
 Source:  Intake 
 Purpose:  Protect  program  integrity  and  inform  supervision 
 Include: 
 ●  Pending  charges 
 ●  Court  obligations 
 ●  Supervision  restrictions 
 ●  Sex  Offender  Registry  requirement  (yes/no) 
 ●  Movement/contact  restrictions 
 This  section  answers: 
 What  legal  constraints  affect  supervision  and  placement? 
 This  section  informs  placement  decisions,  not  treatment  focus. 
 7.  Responsivity  &  Engagement  Profile 
 Source:  LS/CMI  +  professional  judgment 
 Purpose:  Guide  delivery  style 
 Include: 
 ●  Literacy  limitations 
 ●  Cognitive  considerations 
 ●  Trauma  reactivity 
 ●  Authority  sensitivity 
 ●  Motivation  style 
 ●  Learning  preferences 
 This  section  answers: 
 How  should  staff  work  with  this  person  to  be  effective?
