### Week 1: Immediate Stabilization & Transit Setup
- [ ] Confirm daily medication setup through Welvista or Fetter Health Care.
- [ ] Obtain CARTA monthly transit pass and map daily bus travel times.
- [ ] Check in with state probation officer and share your First Shift schedule.

### Week 2: Legal Clarity & Skill Building
- [ ] Attend Father to Father Project intake for driver's license restoration.

### Week 3: Employment Applications & Resume Readiness
- [ ] Finalize resume with your Navigator highlighting cleanup, demolition, and general labor skills.
- [ ] Submit online applications to **Lowcountry Framing** and **Palmetto Logistics**.
- [ ] Practice mock interviews focusing on positive self-talk and professional communication.

### Week 4: Interviews & Career Placement
- [ ] Attend scheduled job interviews with fair-chance hiring partners.
- [ ] Establish a paycheck savings plan for program fees and family support.
- [ ] Celebrate 30-day program milestones with your First Shift support team!
