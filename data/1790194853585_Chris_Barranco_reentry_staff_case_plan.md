# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Christopher Barranco

## 1. Participant Overview
Chris is a 41-year-old male with a strong background in construction, heavy machinery, and forklift operation. He is currently residing in an Oxford House (serving as President) and shows deep commitment to his faith-based recovery. However, he exhibits significant cognitive minimization regarding his criminal history (denying adult convictions and a history of DV) and has a hidden/unacknowledged active probation warrant in Kershaw County.

## 2. Immediate Stability Triage (Red Flags)
- **Active Warrant:** Chris has an active probation warrant out of Kershaw County (Dec 2025). This is an immediate threat to his stability and freedom. 
- **Financial Distress:** Chris is highly stressed about child support, debt ($3,500), and Oxford House rent. 

## 3. Case Management Strategy & CBT Integration
- **Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5):** Chris minimizes his past (denies adult charges/DV). Do not argue. Use reflective listening: *'Chris, I hear that you feel you've left your past behind you. I want to make sure no old records pop up to surprise employers. Let's look at this background report together and you can tell me what's going on with these items.'*
- **Managing Resistance (Standard 3.2):** Link resolving the Kershaw County warrant directly to his self-interest: *'We need to get this warrant cleared up so you don't lose your spot at Oxford House or get pulled off a high-paying construction site.'*
- **Criminogenic Targets:** Address procriminal attitudes (minimization), employment deficits, and ensure his relapse prevention plan remains robust given his history of self-sabotage (Q20).

## 4. Action Items for Navigator
1. **Legal:** Assist Chris in contacting SC Legal Services to determine the nature of the Kershaw warrant. Support him in communicating with PO Agent Burgess.
2. **Financial/Basic Needs:** Refer to Midlands Fatherhood Coalition to adjust child support based on his current unemployed status. Refer to Harvest Hope for food to save his stipend for rent.
3. **Employment:** Prep him for fair-chance industrial/heavy machinery roles. Focus on his gutter installation, Rotochopper, and forklift certifications.
