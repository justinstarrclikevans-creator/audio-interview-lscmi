# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: Dylan Johnson

## 1. Participant Overview
- **Name**: Dylan Johnson
- **Supervision**: Active Probation (Turn90 is mandated)
- **Living Situation**: Rents a room in a transitional house (Stable)
- **Key Strengths**: High self-awareness regarding mental health needs, has a high school diploma, stable housing, strong desire for self-improvement.
- **Primary Barriers**: Emotional reactivity/anger triggers (history of workplace altercation), lack of transportation/driver's license, need for psychiatric support.

## 2. Facilitation & Coaching Strategy
- **Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5)**: Dylan has a history of externalizing blame when he feels disrespected (e.g., the KFC incident). Do not debate who was right or wrong. Use neutral curiosity to explore the consequences of his reactions. *"When you feel ignored, what is your first instinct? How did that reaction impact your goals in the past?"*
- **Managing Resistance (Standard 3.2)**: Tie his program attendance directly to his desire to earn money in warehousing/construction. Acknowledge that riding the bus is frustrating, but frame it as a temporary stepping stone to financial independence.
- **CBT Integration**: Utilize cognitive-behavioral workbooks focusing on impulsive thinking and emotional regulation. Dylan admits he "often acts before thinking" and becomes "impatient with others."

## 3. Immediate Action Items (Next 14 Days)
1. **Transportation**: Issue CARTA bus passes and do a route-planning exercise with him to ensure he can attend Turn90 daily.
2. **Mental Health Triage**: Assist Dylan in setting up an intake appointment at Fetter Health Care Network for a psychiatric evaluation and counseling, as he explicitly requested this help.
3. **Employment Prep**: Review his past warehouse experience (Shimano's) and identify fair-chance regional employers. Begin discussing workplace conflict resolution to prevent a repeat of the KFC incident.
