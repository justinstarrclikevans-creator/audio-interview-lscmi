## 1. Local Community Resources & Referrals

### Driver's License & Parenting Support
* **Center for Fathering / Father to Father Project**
  * **Services:** Assistance with driver's license restoration, child support modification, and parenting guidance.
  * **Contact:** (843) 744-2126 | 4925 Lacross Rd, North Charleston, SC 29406
  * **Links:** [Visit Website](https://scfathersandfamilies.com) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=4925+Lacross+Rd,+North+Charleston,+SC+29406)
  * **Action Step:** Schedule an initial intake appointment to review SCDMV reinstatement requirements.

* **SCDMV Regional Service Center (Leeds Ave)**
  * **Services:** Official driver records, reinstatement fee payment plans, and ID renewals.
  * **Contact:** (803) 896-5000 | 3790 Leeds Ave, North Charleston, SC 29405
  * **Links:** [Visit Website](https://scdmvonline.com/Driver-Services/Reinstatement) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=3790+Leeds+Ave,+North+Charleston,+SC+29405)
  * **Action Step:** Obtain an official driving record printout for license restoration planning.

### Healthcare & Prescription Management
* **South Carolina Healthy Connections Medicaid (SCDHHS)**
  * **Services:** Comprehensive health coverage for doctor visits, mental health, and prescription medications.
  * **Contact:** 1-888-549-0820 | 326 Westinghouse Rd, Charleston, SC 29412
  * **Links:** [Apply Online](https://apply.scdhhs.gov) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=326+Westinghouse+Rd,+Charleston,+SC+29412)
  * **Action Step:** Submit online Medicaid application to ensure continuous coverage for daily prescriptions.

* **Fetter Health Care Network**
  * **Services:** Primary healthcare, sliding-scale dental, and behavioral healthcare.
  * **Contact:** (843) 722-4112 | 51 Nassau St, Charleston, SC 29403
  * **Links:** [Visit Website](https://fetterhealthcare.org) | [Directions](https://maps.google.com/?q=51+Nassau+St,+Charleston,+SC+29403)
  * **Action Step:** Establish care with a primary doctor for ongoing health management.

### Public Transportation
* **CARTA (Charleston Area Regional Transportation Authority)**
  * **Services:** Public bus transit across Charleston, North Charleston, and surrounding industrial hubs.
  * **Contact:** (843) 724-7420
  * **Links:** [Visit Website](https://ridecarta.com)
  * **Action Step:** Obtain monthly bus pass from Navigator and map routes from housing to target employment sites.
