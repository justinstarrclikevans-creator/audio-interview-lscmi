# Re-entry Navigation Case Plan: John Cabrera

## 1. Executive Summary & Assessment Overview
John Cabrera presents with a high risk profile driven primarily by an Antisocial Pattern and Procriminal Attitudes. Key structural strengths include stable housing with his mother and a valid driver's license with reliable transportation. However, significant stability risks exist around untreated PTSD/paranoid schizophrenia, active marijuana use, and non-compliant attitudes regarding firearm possession while on active probation (extending through August 2028).

## 2. Core Criminogenic Target Interventions
* **Procriminal Attitudes:** Utilize CBT workbooks and neutral curiosity questions to address firearm insistence and hypervigilance without escalating resistance.
* **Antisocial Pattern & Interpersonal Reactivity:** Role-play supervisor and peer conflict scenarios emphasizing cognitive pause techniques and workplace survival strategies.
* **Education/Employment Deficits:** Support math GED preparation and route toward fair-chance employers that match his skills (HVAC/trades background).

## 3. Facilitation Strategies & Management Guidelines
* **Modeling Neutrality (Standard 1.5):** Do not debate anti-social statements or firearm logic directly. Acknowledge the underlying feeling of safety concerns while framing compliance in terms of avoiding prison return.
* **Managing Resistance (Standard 3.2 & 18.0):** Pair John with familiar, consistent staff to respect his trust boundary ("circles get you killed") and build steady rapport.
* **Substance Use & CDL Pathway:** Explicitly link drug-free status to passing DOT physicals for his long-term CDL and career goals.

## 4. Immediate Action Steps (30–60 Days)
1. Connect with Charleston Center / Fetter Health Network for voluntary mental health and PTSD trauma support.
2. Initiate math GED tutoring.
3. Match with non-CDL entry-level trades/logistics positions that accept fair-chance candidates.
